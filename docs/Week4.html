Transitivity: how many triads are closed divided by the total amount of triads that are or could have been closed. 300 is fully closed: - last digit: number of dyads without ties - second digit: number of dyads with one tie - first digit: number of dyads with two ties (mutual dyads) - specific subtype: C: cyclic D: downward U: upward T: transitive
